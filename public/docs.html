Workflows.AI

Run customers, quotes, invoicing, inventory and accounting in one place.

Getting started

Set up your organization

Add your company profile, branding and tax details under Configuration.

Add your team

Invite users and assign roles so everyone has the right level of access.

Add customers

Create customer records and contacts — the foundation for quotes and invoices.

Quote, invoice, get paid

Send a quote, convert it to an order, raise the invoice and record the payment.

How-to guides

Create and send a quote
Go to Sales → Quotes and choose New quote. Pick the customer, add line items (products or services) with quantities and prices, then save and send. The quote moves through Draft → Sent → Accepted as the customer responds.
Convert a quote into a sales order
Open an accepted quote and choose Convert to order. The line items carry over into a new sales order, where you can track it from Confirmed through to Delivered.
Raise an invoice and record a payment
From a sales order (or directly under Sales → Invoices), create an invoice. When payment arrives, open the invoice and choose Record payment — the amount posts against the invoice and updates your accounts automatically.
Manage products and stock levels
Under Inventory, add products with a SKU, category and price. Track quantities by warehouse, set reorder points, and record stock movements (receipts, issues, transfers and adjustments).
Set up a workflow automation
Under Automation, create a workflow that runs on a schedule or when an event happens (for example, an invoice becomes overdue). Add actions such as sending an email or notification, or calling a webhook.
Run financial reports
Under Accounting → Reports, run a Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance or General Ledger Detail for any period. Reports can be previewed and exported to PDF.
Environmental compliance

Air Compliance Record Keeping

Log production, track permit limits, and produce the reports your state agency expects.

Getting started

Add your facilities

Create each asphalt plant you operate, including the state it reports to.

Add permits & limits

Enter each facility's permit number, expiration date and monthly emission limits.

Log production daily

Record the day's tonnage, fuel and hours — from the office or a phone at the plant.

Generate & file

Produce the report your agency needs and file it before the deadline.

How-to guides

Add a facility and its permits
Open Facilities and add a plant. Then attach one or more air-emission permits, each with its permit number and expiration date. The facility's active permit is the one valid furthest into the future; you can't remove the last permit or add one that's already expired.
Set monthly permit limits
On a facility, set the monthly limits from your permit — tons, fuel, VOC, NOx, SO₂, CO₂ and HCl. Daily entries are tracked against these limits so you can see how much headroom is left in the month.
Log daily production
Open the facility and add a record for the day: hot mix, warm mix, cold mix, RAP, aggregate, liquid AC, fuel burned and hours run. On idle days, values default to zero — just confirm and save.
Read the compliance dashboard
The dashboard shows each facility's last run date, last record, and upcoming monthly and quarterly filing dates — color-coded by urgency — plus an at-a-glance status so nothing slips.
Generate a report for filing
Under Reports, choose a template — such as the Annual Emissions Inventory, an opacity (Method 9) log, a stack-test summary, an SPCC review or a stormwater inspection — pick the period, and generate a PDF ready to submit to your state agency.
