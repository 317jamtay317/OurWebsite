About us

An independent software business.

ProManager Online builds practical business software and develops custom applications for companies that need something built to fit. We make tools we'd want to use ourselves, and we stand behind them.

What we do

We earn our revenue two ways, and both are simple:

  • Software subscriptions. Customers subscribe to software we build, host and maintain — Workflows.AI for running a small business, and Air Compliance Record Keeping for asphalt-plant emissions reporting.
  • Custom development. We build bespoke web and business applications for clients, billed per project as milestones are delivered.

How we charge

Every payment we accept is a customer paying us directly for our own software or for development work we perform for them. To be completely clear about what we are and are not:

We are a software company — not a financial service. We are not a lender, we do not offer loans, credit or installment financing, we do not hold funds in escrow, and we do not process payments on behalf of third parties.
