Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 4 June 2026

We want you to be happy with what you pay for. This policy explains how cancellation and refunds work for our software subscriptions and our custom development projects.

Software subscriptions

  • Cancel any time. You can cancel your subscription whenever you like. Your plan stays active until the end of the current paid period and then will not renew.
  • 14-day satisfaction window. If you're a new subscriber and you're not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund that payment.
  • Renewals. After the first period, charges for periods already started are generally non-refundable, except where required by law.
  • No financing. Subscriptions are simple recurring payments for access to the software — there is no loan, credit or installment plan involved.

Custom development projects

  • Custom work is quoted and delivered in milestones, and invoiced as each milestone is completed.
  • Because each invoice covers development work already performed and delivered, completed milestones are non-refundable.
  • If a project is stopped partway through, you are billed only for work delivered up to that point. We do not hold funds in advance or in escrow.
  • Any specific arrangements for a project are set out in that project's written agreement.

How to cancel or request a refund

Email support@promanageronline.com with your account email and details of your request. We'll confirm and process eligible refunds back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
