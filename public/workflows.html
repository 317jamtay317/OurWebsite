Workflows.AI

Run your whole business in one place.

Workflows.AI brings your customers, quotes, invoices, inventory and books together in one system — with automation that takes care of the routine work. Built to be right-sized for small contractors and teams.

Features

Everything a small team needs to run the day

From the first quote to a paid invoice and tidy books — without juggling five different apps.

Customers & CRM

Track leads, contacts and deals in a simple pipeline, and keep a clean record of every customer and conversation.

Quotes & orders

Send professional quotes, turn the approved ones into orders in a click, and never lose track of what's been agreed.

Invoicing & payments

Raise invoices, record payments, and always know who owes you what and when it's due.

Inventory & stock

Manage products, stock levels and suppliers across one or more locations, with reorder points and purchase orders.

Accounting

A real double-entry ledger with chart of accounts, journal entries, bank reconciliation and reports — P&L, balance sheet and trial balance.

Workflow automation

Set up scheduled and event-triggered automations — send emails, post updates and trigger actions — so routine steps happen on their own.

Also includes role-based access and permissions, an optional online storefront, and a built-in report designer. Available as a web app and a Windows desktop app.
