FAQ

Frequently asked questions

A clear, plain-English explanation of what we sell and how paying us works.

What does ProManager Online sell?
Two things: subscriptions to our own software products — Workflows.AI (all-in-one business management) and Air Compliance Record Keeping (air-emissions compliance for asphalt plants) — and custom software development services where we build applications for other businesses.
Who am I paying, and what for?
You pay ProManager Online directly, either for a software subscription or for development work we perform for you. There is no third party involved — your payment goes to us, for our software and our work.
Do you offer loans, credit or financing?
No. We are a software company, not a lender or financial service. We do not offer loans, credit, "buy now pay later", or installment financing of any kind.
Do you hold money in escrow or handle payments for others?
No. We do not hold customer funds in escrow, and we never process or facilitate payments on behalf of third parties. Every payment is simply a customer paying us for our own products and services.
What does "milestone billing" mean for custom projects?
For custom development, we agree a scope and split it into milestones. As we complete and deliver each milestone, we invoice you for that work. It's simply a way to pay for development in sensible stages — each invoice is payment for software work already delivered. It is not financing, not a payment plan, and no funds are held in escrow.
Can I cancel my subscription? Do you offer refunds?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time and it won't renew for the next period. See our Refunds & Cancellation policy for full details.
How are payments processed?
Card and online payments are handled securely by our payment processor. We don't store full card numbers ourselves. Your statement will show a descriptor identifying ProManager Online.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Access is encrypted, and our products use role-based permissions so only the right people in your team can see your data. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle information.
How do I get support?
Email support@promanageronline.com or use our contact form. We usually reply within one business day.

Still have a question?

We're happy to explain anything about our products, services or billing.
