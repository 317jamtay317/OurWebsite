How projects work

A clear scope, delivered in milestones

Every project starts with an agreed scope. We break the work into milestones, and you're invoiced for development work as each milestone is completed and delivered.

Scope & quote

We agree on what we're building and send a written quote that lays out the milestones and their cost.

Build a milestone

We develop the agreed piece of work and keep you updated on progress as we go.

Deliver & invoice

When a milestone is delivered, we invoice you for that completed development work.

Continue or wrap up

We move to the next milestone, and finish when the agreed scope is complete.

Plain and simple: you pay for work delivered

Milestone invoicing is just a way to pay for development in sensible stages instead of one lump sum. Each invoice is payment for software development work we've already performed. We do not offer financing or installment credit, we do not hold your money in escrow, and we do not handle payments for any third party. It's a direct, professional services arrangement between you and us.
