Air Compliance Record Keeping

Air-emissions compliance, without the spreadsheets.

Purpose-built for hot-mix asphalt plants. Log daily production, track your permit limits automatically, and produce the records and reports your state agency expects — across every facility, from the office or the field.

Why it exists

Stop assembling compliance by hand

Most plants still track daily tonnage, fuel and emissions on paper or in fragile spreadsheets, then scramble to pull it all together at filing time. One transposed number or missed log can turn into a deviation. Air Compliance Record Keeping keeps the record straight all year, so filing is just a click — not a fire drill.

Features

Built around how asphalt plants actually report

Daily production logging

Record hot mix, warm mix, cold mix, RAP, aggregate, liquid AC, fuel burned and hours run — quickly, every day, with idle days defaulting to zero.

Permit-limit tracking

Set your monthly permit limits — tons, fuel, VOC, NOx, SO₂, CO₂ and HCl — and the system tracks every entry against them so you stay inside your permit.

Every facility, every state

Manage all your plants from one login, with a unified compliance view — even when facilities operate under different permits and in different states.

State-ready reports

Generate the filings you need — annual emissions inventory, opacity (Method 9) logs, stack-test summaries, SPCC reviews and stormwater inspections — as clean PDFs.

Deadline dashboard

See what's due and when, color-coded by urgency, so monthly and annual filings never sneak up on you.

Audit-ready records

Your history is stored securely and kept intact, so when an inspector asks, the records are right there — accurate and complete.

Supports state agencies including Indiana's IDEM, with multi-state operation. Works on phones and tablets for entry right at the plant.
