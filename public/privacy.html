Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProManager Online ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, subscribe to our software, or engage our development services.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact information — such as your name, email address and company details when you sign up or contact us.
  • Service data — the information you enter into our Software to run your business (projects, customers, work items, and similar).
  • Payment information — handled by our third-party payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as pages visited and device/browser type, used to keep the service reliable and secure.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve our Software and Services.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription or project.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.
  • To send service-related communications (for example, billing or important changes).
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — such as our payment processor and hosting provider — and only as needed to deliver the service, or where required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies necessary for it to function. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

Data security

We use encrypted connections and role-based access controls to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to safeguard your data.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, then for any period required to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@promanageronline.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted here with a revised "last updated" date.
